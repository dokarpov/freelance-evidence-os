What is real here?

The scenario models the logic of Rilatu's private evidence-engine experiments: claim dependencies, recipient-specific disclosures and invalidation after a source revision. This website runs a transparent state-machine simulation. It does not perform signatures, verify documents, contact banks or check a live issuer. The private engineering prototype has its own tests; this public demo is not that executable cryptographic implementation.
