05GOOD TO KNOW

Before you trust a prototype.

Does this calculate or file taxes?

No. This is an interactive research concept. It does not process real documents, calculate taxes or prepare filings.

Can an expense be deducted in three countries?

Not automatically. The three country views illustrate different possible review contexts, not three simultaneous deductions. Actual treatment depends on the taxpayer and transaction.

Can I upload my own documents?

No. Everything on this page uses synthetic examples in your browser; there is no backend or personal document storage.

What is the product stage?

Interactive research concept. The next milestone is validating workflows with freelancers and qualified accountants before building live document processing.
