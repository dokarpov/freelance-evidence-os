05 / THE FINE PRINT

Good questions
deserve answers.

Does this calculate or file my taxes?

No. This is an interactive concept demonstrating evidence capture and review. It does not process actual financial records or prepare legally valid filings.

Can one receipt be deducted in three countries?

Not automatically. The three views illustrate different review contexts, not three simultaneous deductions. Actual treatment depends on the taxpayer, transaction, jurisdiction, and tax regime.

Can I upload private documents here?

No. The demo uses a synthetic invoice entirely in your browser. There is no document upload, storage, AI analysis, or connected accounting service on this website.

What is the product's current stage?

Research and interactive concept. The next milestone is validating the evidence-review workflow with freelancers and qualified accountants before building a production tax rules engine.
