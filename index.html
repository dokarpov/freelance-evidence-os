05GOOD TO KNOW

Before you trust a prototype.

Does this certify or verify income?

No. This demonstration only links synthetic records. It does not authenticate files, certify income or guarantee acceptance.

Does one payment prove stable income?

No. One matched payment is evidence for one period, not proof of income continuity. More history and independent verification would be needed.

Can I upload my own documents?

No. Everything on this page uses synthetic examples in your browser; there is no backend or personal document storage.

What is the product stage?

Interactive research concept. The next milestone is validating source-linking and evidence-review workflows with freelancers and the people receiving their documents.
